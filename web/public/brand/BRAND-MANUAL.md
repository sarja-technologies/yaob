# Fylume & Yaob / Brand manual

## The relationship

Fylume is the lab. Yaob is a model family from the lab. Use Fylume for research, company communication and the root domain. Use Yaob for model documentation and product identity. In prose write “Fylume” and “Yaob”; the visual wordmarks use “fylu.me” and “yaob”. The URL is fylu.me. The optional fylume-name SVG is for contexts that call for the lab name without the domain punctuation.

## Fylume

The Prism symbol is an eight-part refractive aperture. Each solid facet turns between its inner and outer edge, creating a clear open centre and a compact circular silhouette. There is no letter or monogram. The construction is geometric, not a neural-network diagram. It works in a single flat colour. Black is the canvas; white is the light. The glow is atmosphere behind a sharply rendered mark. Do not blur the actual outline.

Colour roles: Void #050505 for pages; Surface #111111 for elevated regions; Line #303030 for separators; Muted #ABABAB for secondary text; White #F7F7F2 for text and marks; Glow #FFFFFF for the restrained light effect. Use white and black as the overwhelming majority of the composition. Avoid added accent colours.

Use Space Grotesk 500 for display, 400 for paragraphs and 500–600 for action labels. Desktop display 72/76 px, section headings 40/44, subheads 24/30, body 17/28, labels 13/18. Mobile display 44/48, section headings 32/36, body 16/26. Display tracking -0.05em, heading tracking -0.03em, body tracking normal. IBM Plex Mono 400 at 11/16 is reserved for technical labels and code.

## Yaob

The Membrane symbol develops the three-cell motif into three hollow, rounded forms. Each contains a curved seed and the full symbol surrounds one shared central seed. It nods to the Three Cell Node idea while remaining an identifying mark, not a technical schematic. The regular master includes the inner seeds; the micro master removes them. The lowercase wordmark is drawn from custom paths with rounded terminals, open y geometry and circular a/o/b counters. The wordmark is artwork; typing the name in Manrope does not recreate it.

Colour roles: Ink #2A1C16 for text and primary buttons; Paper #FFF3DC for pages; Ember #F34B27 for the symbol, feature surfaces and large accents; Gold #F7B547 for small decorative details; Clay #8F3B24 for secondary emphasis and button hover. Lead with paper, ink and ember. Use the multicolour symbol as a secondary editorial or large display treatment; use a single-colour version for small sizes.

Use Manrope 650 for display, 400–450 for paragraphs and 600 for labels. Desktop display 64/70 px, section headings 36/42, subheads 24/30, body 17/28, labels 13/18. Mobile display 44/49, section headings 30/36, body 16/26. Display tracking -0.045em, heading tracking -0.025em, body normal. Use IBM Plex Mono for technical labels.

## Logo handling

Clear space: at least 25% of the visible symbol height around standalone marks and full lockups. Standalone hero wordmarks need at least 50% of letter height above and below. The file's transparent padding is not the entire required clear space.

Minimum digital sizes: wordmark 100 px wide; horizontal lockup 160 px wide. When a lockup makes Yaob’s symbol smaller than 40 px, replace its detailed symbol with the corresponding micro master while preserving proportions. Use Fylume from 24 px and the detailed Yaob mark from 40 px. From 16–32 px use the dedicated micro SVGs or supplied favicon artwork, with no glow. The micro Yaob mark removes inner seeds and preserves the three membranes and central seed. For print use symbols at least 8 mm high and wordmarks at least 25 mm wide; proof at actual size.

Use white Fylume artwork on dark backgrounds and black on light backgrounds. Use ink or ember Yaob artwork on paper, and cream artwork on ink or ember. Do not stretch, skew, rotate, outline, recolour, add gradients to, or typeset over the marks. Preserve the symbol/wordmark proportions in lockups. Do not combine Fylume's symbol with Yaob's wordmark.

Glow recipe: two very faint neutral-white shadows at 6 px / 44% opacity and 20 px / 19% opacity. Static by default. Keep the core mark sharp. Avoid an oversized fog cloud and keep glow away from paragraphs or technical information. Flat artwork is the default for print, small UI and favicon uses.

## Layout

Use an 8 px spacing grid with 4 px for fine alignment. Desktop content width 1280 px; side margins 64–96 px. Mobile side margins 20–24 px. Section spacing 96–128 px desktop, 56–72 px mobile. Fylume uses squared corners (0–2 px), thin rules and generous negative space. Yaob uses 24 px corners on large surfaces, 12 px on small surfaces and pill buttons. Use shape sparingly so content remains the focus.

## Colour contrast

Use the contrast report in this folder for measured WCAG contrast ratios. Body text: Fylume White or Muted on Void/Surface; Yaob Ink on Paper, Ember or Gold; Paper on Ink or Clay. Paper text on Ember is not a normal-text pairing. Ember text on Paper is restricted to large text and graphics. Gold on Paper is decoration only. Logos are separate from reading text; ensure surrounding navigation, controls and captions remain legible. Do not use colour alone to convey state.

## Voice

Direct, curious and lightly irreverent. Prefer concrete, ordinary language. Direction examples: “Think freely. Build lightly.” “Your ideas. Your machine.” “Intelligence, closer to home.” “Room for your next idea.” These are brand lines, not performance claims. Avoid claiming all models are free or open source until release licences establish that. Avoid claiming a microwave or a particular computer can run a model without real verification.

## Export and reproduction

SVG is the master for websites, vector editors and print handoff. PNG is a convenience export in sRGB, 2048 px longest edge. Fonts and logos are separate assets. No external network request is necessary to view the kit. GPL files are palette swatches for GIMP and compatible applications, not software licences. Keep original font OFL files with font redistribution.
